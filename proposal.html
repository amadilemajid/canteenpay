RE: PROPOSAL TO INTRODUCE CANTEENPAY — A CASHLESS CANTEEN WALLET SYSTEM FOR YOUR SCHOOL

Dear Sir / Madam,

We write to formally introduce CanteenPay, a simple, secure, and locally built digital wallet system designed specifically for Ugandan boarding and day schools. CanteenPay eliminates the need for students to carry cash on school premises by enabling parents to top up their child's canteen wallet remotely via MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money, and allowing students to spend at the canteen using only their Student ID and a 4-digit PIN.

StepWhoWhat Happens
1 Parent Visits the school's top-up page, enters the student's registration number and amount, then approves the Mobile Money prompt on their phone.
2 System Wallet is credited instantly. Parent receives an SMS receipt. No cash changes hands.
3 Student At the canteen, the cashier scans the student's QR code (or types the ID), enters the amount, and the student confirms with their PIN.
4 Bursar Views a real-time daily settlement report showing all sales, top-ups, and platform fees — printable in one click.
Key Benefits for Your School:
  • ✅ No cash handling — eliminates theft, loss, and end-of-day cash reconciliation errors.
  • ✅ Daily spending limits — school sets a per-student daily cap (e.g. UGX 10,000) to prevent overspending.
  • ✅ Works with existing Student IDs — no new cards or hardware required beyond a tablet or smartphone at the canteen.
  • ✅ Instant parent visibility — parents receive SMS confirmation every time money is loaded or spent.
  • ✅ Built for Uganda — supports MTN MoMo & Airtel Money, UGX currency, and local phone number formats.

All we ask is your cooperation in running the pilot and providing feedback. After the pilot, the only cost is a flat UGX 500 per top-up transaction — paid by the parent, not the school.

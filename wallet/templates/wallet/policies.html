CanteenPay Uganda — Terms & Policies

Effective for all schools, parents, and canteen operators using CanteenPay Uganda.

1. Terms of Service

CanteenPay Uganda is a cashless school canteen wallet and pocket-money management platform. Parents top up their child’s wallet via Mobile Money (MTN MoMo or Airtel Money), and funds settle directly into the participating school’s bank account.

  • Platform fee: A flat UGX 500 convenience fee is added to every top-up transaction and retained by CanteenPay Uganda for platform operation and support.
  • Direct settlement: When a school has a Flutterwave subaccount configured, the student’s portion settles directly to the school’s bank account while the platform fee is retained separately.
  • Minimum top-up: UGX 1,000 per transaction.
  • Daily spending limit: Each school sets a daily spend limit per student. Canteen purchases cannot exceed the remaining daily limit.
  • By using CanteenPay, you authorize Mobile Money charges and confirm you are the parent or legal guardian of the registered student.

2. Refund & Dispute Policy

If a Mobile Money top-up is not credited to a student’s wallet, or if a duplicate charge occurs:

  • Transaction reference: Every top-up generates a unique reference ID. Keep this reference (shown on your receipt or SMS) for any dispute.
  • Verification: The school bursar or CanteenPay support verifies the transaction status directly with Flutterwave using the reference ID.
  • Resolution time: Verified uncredited or duplicate top-ups are resolved within 24 hours—either credited to the student’s wallet or refunded to the parent’s Mobile Money account.
  • Contact: Email amadilemajid10@gmail.com with the transaction reference, student registration ID, and amount.

3. Privacy Policy

CanteenPay Uganda protects personal data in compliance with Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act.

  • Student registration IDs: Used only to link top-ups and canteen purchases to the correct student wallet. Never shared with third parties.
  • Parent phone numbers: Collected solely to send Mobile Money top-up prompts and SMS receipts. Not used for marketing without consent.
  • 4-digit canteen PINs: Encrypted and stored securely. PINs are required at the canteen point of sale to authorize purchases and are never transmitted in plain text.
  • Access control: Only the school bursar and authorized staff can view transaction and settlement reports.
